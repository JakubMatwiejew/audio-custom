— Why replace

When restoration is not enough.

The RE-301 adds chorus to the tape echo and a busier panel to go with it. Decades of use leave the legends worn and the surface scratched or corroded.

When restoring the original is no longer an option, a new faceplate brings back the look of the unit without changing anything inside it.

— FAQ

Before you order.

Will it fit my unit?

Send us a photo of your current panel and the details from the back of the unit. We'll confirm the fit before you order.

Is fitting included?

The panel ships on its own. Fitting it means opening the unit, so we recommend a technician if you're not comfortable doing that yourself.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. About 99% of our work goes abroad. Panels are packed for long-distance transit.
