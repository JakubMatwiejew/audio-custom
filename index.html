— About

A workshop where vintage instruments find their second life.

It started in 2013 with an Akai MPC on the bench. More than a decade later, Audio Custom has turned into a workshop dedicated to one thing — the visual side of legendary instruments.

We restore corroded, scratched and bent panels back to factory condition. Aluminium side cheeks for the Jupiter-8, wooden cheeks for the MPC 3000, full faceplates for Roland Space Echo 201 and 301 — each piece is straightened, refinished and finished by hand.

Or, if you prefer, we go the other way: a custom design in a colour of your choosing. The instrument stays the same. The way it looks on stage doesn't.

We work with private collectors, with musicians who want to keep their gear playing for another forty years, with recording studios — and with hardware makers who need finished panels for the synths they're building today.

Every job is hand-finished. No two pieces leave the shop identical, because no two pieces arrive that way.

— Lost parts, remade

The knob that broke in 1987 can be made again.

Many small parts of vintage instruments have been out of production for decades. We measure an original, model it and reproduce it with 3D printing, then finish it to sit naturally next to the original parts.

01

Send the part

The broken piece, a matching part from another unit, or clear photos with measurements.

02

We model it

The part is measured and rebuilt as a 3D model. Broken or missing sections are reconstructed.

03

Test print

A first print is checked for fit against the instrument before anything else is made.

04

Finished part

Printed and finished in the colour of the original. One piece or a full set.

Knobs, data wheels, LCD housings, slider caps, buttons, battery covers, brackets, feet. If it's missing, ask.

— FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. About 99% of our work goes abroad: to clients across Europe, North America, Asia and Australia. Panels and parts are packed for long-distance transit and we agree the shipping method with you for each job. Distance is not a problem, it's how we work every day.

Do you repair electronics?

No. We work on the visual side only: panels, side cheeks, enclosures and small parts. Electronic repairs stay with your technician, and we're happy to work alongside them.

Do I need to send the whole instrument?

Usually not. Most jobs need only the panel, the side cheeks or the part itself. Tell us the model and the job, and we'll say exactly what to send.

How long does it take?

It depends on the job and the queue. You get a timeline together with the quote, before anything is sent.

How much does it cost?

Every job is quoted individually, because every panel arrives in a different condition. Send photos and we'll give you a price.

Can you make panels or enclosures for my own instrument?

Yes, from a single prototype to a production run of a few hundred pieces. A sketch with dimensions is enough. We handle cutting, bending, standoffs, powder coating and printing. Tell us the quantity you have in mind. More for makers

Can you reproduce a part I no longer have?

Often yes. We need something to measure: a matching part from another unit, or clear photos with dimensions. How part reproduction works
