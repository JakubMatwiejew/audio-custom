— FAQ

About parts.

What can you reproduce?

Small plastic parts that are out of production: knobs, data wheels, slider caps, buttons, battery covers, brackets, feet and housings.

I don't have the original part. Can you still help?

Often yes. We need something to measure: a matching part from another unit, or clear photos with dimensions.

Do you make one piece or a set?

Both. One missing knob or a full set for an instrument.
